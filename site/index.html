About this sound map

Sounds of the Adriatic is a listening gallery of the Croatian coast: a dark map with pins, each pin a field recording of that place — harbours, pine coves, wind in the rigging, church bells, open-sea swell. Nothing plays until you tap a pin.

Every published sound is licensed CC0 or CC-BY and credited on its pin card — non-commercial and no-derivatives licenses are never used here. The coastline is Natural Earth (public domain). Recordings come from Freesound (CC0/CC-BY only) and from the maker's own trips along the coast. The full machine-readable list — creator, license URL, source URL, verification date — is in LICENSE-MANIFEST.json and on the credits page.

Questions

Are the recordings free to use?

Yes. Every published sound is CC0 or CC-BY — the only licenses we ship. Non-commercial (CC-BY-NC) and no-derivatives (CC-BY-ND) licenses are banned here, because this project promotes a commercial product. To reuse a recording, credit the creator and link the license shown on its pin card; the full list is on the credits page and in LICENSE-MANIFEST.json.

Where was each sound actually recorded?

Each pin card and the manifest carry the exact coordinates and recording date, and the caption states the real place. Some Adriatic recordings were made on the Italian or Slovenian shore — the sea has no border — and every caption says where the sound was actually made.

What is the sea organ in Zadar?

The Morske orgulje is a wave-powered sound installation built into the Zadar waterfront: the sea pushes air through tuned pipes. Its pin plays a CC0 recording of the organ at the Zadar seafront — the recordist notes it is mixed with the voices of passers-by, as on a busy summer day.

Can I contribute a recording?

Yes, if it is CC0 or CC-BY. Ideally 30–90 seconds, mono, no talking, wind guard on the mic. Open an issue or PR in the project repository with the file, exact location, date and license — nothing is published until the license is verified.

How do I link to or share one sound?

Use the pin's own page, e.g. sound.marespot.com/s/senj-bura.html, or the map deep link sound.marespot.com/#sound=senj-bura (also accepted as ?sound=senj-bura). Please credit the creator and license when you share.
